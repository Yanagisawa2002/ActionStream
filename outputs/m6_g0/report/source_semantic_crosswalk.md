## RTC boundary

RTC does remove a measured-delay prefix when its opt-in queue replaces a chunk ([`RTCInferenceEngine._rtc_loop`](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/62600065cdb349c1e41b0403c511f12ebfa686eb/src/lerobot/rollout/inference/rtc.py#L288-L327), [`ActionQueue.merge, _replace_actions_queue`](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/62600065cdb349c1e41b0403c511f12ebfa686eb/src/lerobot/policies/rtc/action_queue.py#L147-L188)). However, the rollout gate requires a policy declaration and an extended `predict_action_chunk(..., inference_delay, prev_chunk_left_over)` call shape ([`supports_rtc_inference`](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/62600065cdb349c1e41b0403c511f12ebfa686eb/src/lerobot/rollout/inference/rtc.py#L66-L80), [`make_rollout_context RTC guard`](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/62600065cdb349c1e41b0403c511f12ebfa686eb/src/lerobot/rollout/context.py#L227-L238)). `ACTPolicy.predict_action_chunk(batch)` has neither ([`ACTPolicy.predict_action_chunk`](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/62600065cdb349c1e41b0403c511f12ebfa686eb/src/lerobot/policies/act/modeling_act.py#L125-L135)); therefore RTC was not forced into the ACT-compatible synthetic matrix.
